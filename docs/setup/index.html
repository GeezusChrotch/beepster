Connect Beepster

Beepster talks to the Beeper Desktop app through your own private Mac gateway. Enter the private HTTPS address shown during Mac setup.

  1. Keep Beeper Desktop and the Beepster companion running on your Mac.
  2. Connect this phone and the Mac to the same Tailscale network.
  3. Paste the private address ending in /configure.

This page does not receive your Beeper token, messages, pairing code, or gateway credential. Your browser goes directly to your Mac after you continue.
